How it works in 30 seconds

  1. Pick an account. Choose a size, from $5k to $50k.
  2. Follow the rules. Don't lose more than your limits allow.
  3. Hit the target. Reach the profit goal and you're funded.
  4. Get paid. Claim your share of the profits.

What sets your price

With the Customizer, you pick the rules. Easier rules cost more. Harder rules cost less.

Account size

How much money you trade. Bigger account, bigger price.

Profit target

How much you need to make to pass. A lower target is easier, so it costs more.

Max drawdown

How far your account can drop before it closes. More room costs more.

Daily loss limit

The most you can lose in one day. No limit costs more. A tighter limit costs less.

Profit split

The share of profits you keep. A bigger share costs more.

Tip: on a $25k account, a 3% daily limit means you can lose up to $750 in a day.

Glossary

Evaluation
A test phase. Hit the profit target without breaking the rules and you get funded.
Funded account
The account you trade after passing. You keep your share of the profits you claim.
Profit target
How much you need to make to pass.
Max drawdown (floor)
The lowest your balance can go. Hit it and the account closes.
Daily loss limit
The most you can lose in one day.
Profit split
Your share of the profit you claim.
Claim Profit
How you take a payout from a funded account.
Fail Price
Shown on the order form. It's the price where this trade would hit your floor.
Leverage
Lets a small balance control a bigger position. Losses get bigger too.

How the accounts fit together

Evaluation accounts (Customizer and 2-Step) are a test. Pass, and you get a funded account. Instant skips the test: you're funded from day one, for a higher fee.

Rookie mistakes

  • Oversizing: positions too big for your floor.
  • Revenge trading after a loss.
  • Trading straight through big news.
  • Forgetting the daily loss limit.

Big news days

CPI, FOMC and jobs reports can move prices fast. Know when they land and size down or sit out. This is education, not advice.
