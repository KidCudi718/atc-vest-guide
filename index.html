FAQ

What does code ATC do?

It takes 5% off the one-time fee on Vest Capital accounts. It's applied at checkout. If you don't see it, type ATC in the Discount Code box.

Do I need to deposit my own money?

No. You pay a one-time fee and Vest Capital funds the account. Funded accounts can't receive deposits.

Do I keep the profits from the evaluation?

No. Trading during the evaluation is simulated. Once you pass and claim your funded account, profit can be claimed.

How do I get paid?

Use Claim Profit to move your share from your funded account to your Primary Account. Each claim processes in 24 hours. From your Primary Account you can withdraw to an external wallet at any time.

What's the one rule that closes an account?

Max drawdown. It's a fixed loss floor set when the account is created. If your account equity drops through it, the account closes right away. A daily loss limit is optional on custom accounts, and evaluations add a profit target to pass.

Can I get a refund?

Vest offers a refund within 3 days of purchase if no trades have been placed on the account. Ask through the in-platform chat or at support@vestmarkets.com.

Can I sign up with only a crypto wallet?

Yes. Vest lets you register with an email address, a Google account or a crypto wallet.

Is this Vest's website?

No. This is an unofficial guide by All Things Crypto. We're Vest affiliates. For anything about your account, contact Vest at support@vestmarkets.com.

Glossary
Evaluation
A test phase. Hit the profit target without breaking the rules and you get funded.
Funded account
The account you trade after passing. You keep your share of the profits you claim.
Profit target
How much you need to make to pass.
Max drawdown (floor)
The lowest your balance can go. Hit it and the account closes.
Daily loss limit
The most you can lose in one day.
Profit split
Your share of the profit you claim.
Claim Profit
How you take a payout from a funded account.
Fail Price
Shown on the order form. It's the price where this trade would hit your floor.
Leverage
Lets a small balance control a bigger position. Losses get bigger too.
Perp
A perpetual contract: it tracks a market's price and has no expiry date.
Rookie mistakes and big news days
  • Oversizing: positions too big for your floor.
  • Revenge trading after a loss.
  • Trading straight through big news.
  • Forgetting the daily loss limit.

CPI, FOMC and jobs reports can move prices fast. Know when they land and size down or sit out. This is education, not advice.
