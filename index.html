Glossary

Evaluation
A test phase. Hit the profit target without breaking the rules and you get funded.
Funded account
The account you trade after passing. Profits you claim are split with you.
Profit target
How much you need to make to pass, as a percent of the account.
Max drawdown (floor)
The lowest your balance can go. Touch it and the account closes.
Daily loss limit
The most you can lose in one day.
Profit split
Your share of the profit you claim.
Claim Profit
How you take a payout from a funded account.
Fail Price
Shown on the order form: the price where this trade would push you to your floor.
Leverage
Lets a small balance control a bigger position. It magnifies losses too.

How the accounts fit together

Evaluation accounts (Customizer and 2-Step) are a test. Pass, and you get a funded account. Instant skips the test: you're funded from day one, for a higher fee.

Rookie mistakes

  • Oversizing: too big a position for your floor.
  • Revenge trading after a loss.
  • Trading straight through big news.
  • Forgetting the daily loss limit.

Big news days

CPI, FOMC and jobs reports can move prices fast. Know when they land and size down or sit out. This is education, not advice.
